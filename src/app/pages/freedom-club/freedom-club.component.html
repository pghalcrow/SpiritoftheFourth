Freedom Club

Support San Diego's oldest and largest Independence Day celebration by joining the Freedom Club today. This patriotic event is organized year-round by an entirely all-volunteer, community supported nonprofit organization.

Donations of $150.00 and over secure membership in our Freedom Club, with preferential parking and seating for the parade and fireworks, tickets to our VIP Reception on July 4th, as well as invitations to Freedom Club events.
