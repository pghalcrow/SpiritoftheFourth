Community Fair

The Spirit of the Fourth Community Fair is a fun and exciting family event in beautiful Webb Lake Park.

The Community Fair offers a wide array of food and beverages, children's activities, nonprofit organizations, arts and crafts, and local businesses.

Enjoy snow cones and other tasty treats while wandering through the Wheels of Freedom Motor Show with over 120 classic cars on display.

An assortment of delicious and tantalizing food will be available from our food vendors and food trucks, so come have a great lunch before getting in place for the Spirit of the Fourth Parade.
