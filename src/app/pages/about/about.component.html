The Spirit of the Fourth is an all volunteer, nonprofit organization that plans and orchestrates Rancho Bernardo's annual 4th of July celebration, as well as many other family oriented events throughout the year.

The Spirit of the Fourth committee comprises volunteers who work year-round to plan the Independenece Day celebration and other events in Rancho Bernardo. The are joined by many other volunteers devoted to making all the events enjoyable for everyone.
