Help us to make the Spirit of the Fourth Celebration a fantastic event!

The Spirit of the Fourth is an all-volunteer nonprofit organization that works year round to provide Rancho Bernardo and the surrounding area with its annual Independence Day celebration. We Invite volunteers to serve on the board in planning and coordinating the event, serve on committees and work day-of events. Whether you're able to volunteer from home or onsite, year-round or one day, we would love to have you as part of our volunteer team.

Are you part of a group or organization that would be interested in getting involved? We would love to have you. We work with all sorts of groups from church youth groups to Boy Scouts to The Rotary Club.
