> Wheels of Freedom Motor Show

Highlighting classic and vintage cars, the Wheels of Freedom Motor Show epitomizes America's love of beautiful cars and trucks. See cool cars, have a chance to interact with their owners and hear their stories. The motor show opens at 9:00AM and is organized and hosted by the Poway Cruisers.

Do you have a classic car that you would like to show off? Fill out our entry form. Entries are limited to 140 cars so register today, entry fee is $25.00.
